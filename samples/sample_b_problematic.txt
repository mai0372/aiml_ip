STANDARD RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Indenture of Lease is made on this 15th day of November 2026, at Pune, Maharashtra,

BETWEEN:
Mr. Vikram Malhotra, residing at Bungalow 12, Koregaon Park, Pune (hereinafter called the "Landlord") of the FIRST PART;

AND:
Mr. Rohan Mehta, residing at Kothrud, Pune (hereinafter called the "Tenant") of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS the Landlord agrees to let and the Tenant agrees to take on rent Apartment 5B, Skyline Towers, Viman Nagar, Pune.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS:

1. Tenancy Period
The tenancy shall be for a term of eleven (11) months commencing on 1st December 2026.

2. Monthly Rent
The Tenant shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 30,000/- (Rupees Thirty Thousand only), payable in advance on or before the 1st of every month.

3. Early Termination by Tenant
The Tenant is expressly permitted to terminate this agreement at any time prior to the expiry of the term by serving thirty (30) days advance written notice to the Landlord.

4. Security Deposit
The Tenant shall pay and deposit with the Landlord an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 1,80,000/- (Rupees One Lakh Eighty Thousand only), being equivalent to six (6) months of monthly rent.

5. Permitted Use
The premises shall be used for private residential living only and the Tenant shall not sublet or assign possession.

6. Electricity and Water Charges
The Tenant shall pay all charges for consumption of electricity and water according to individual sub-meter readings.

7. Domestic Pets
The Tenant is permitted to keep domestic companion pets including dogs and cats within the premises, provided no nuisance is caused to neighbors.

8. Repairs and Maintenance
The Tenant shall keep the interior of the premises in good order and repair at his own cost.

9. Landlord Right of Entry
The Landlord or his representatives reserve the unrestricted right to enter and inspect the premises at any time without notice as deemed necessary.

10. Rent Revision
The Landlord reserves the right to increase the monthly rent at his sole discretion without advance notice.

11. Lock-in Period
There shall be an initial lock-in period of six (6) months.

12. Prohibition on Short Notice Termination
Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, early termination of the tenancy by the tenant with less than ninety (90) days notice is strictly prohibited and forbidden under all circumstances.

13. Surrender on Expiry
Upon the expiry or lawful determination of the tenancy, the Tenant shall peacefully vacate and hand over keys to the Landlord.

14. Remedy for Delayed Rent and Utility Disconnection
If the monthly rent is delayed by more than seven days, the Landlord shall have the right to disconnect electricity and water supply to the premises without further notice or legal recourse.

15. Total Deposit Forfeiture
In case of any breach or premature departure, the entire security deposit of Rs. 1,80,000 shall be automatically forfeited as liquidated damages.

16. Absolute Ban on Animals and Pets
The Tenant is strictly forbidden from bringing, keeping, or harboring any animals, dogs, birds, or pets of any kind on the premises under penalty of immediate eviction.
