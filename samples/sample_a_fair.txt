RESIDENTIAL HOUSE RENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement of House Rent is made on this 1st day of October 2026, at Bengaluru, Karnataka,

BETWEEN:
Mr. Anand Sharma, residing at Flat 402, Green Meadows, Indiranagar, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Landlord", which expression shall include his heirs and assigns) of the ONE PART;

AND:
Ms. Priya Nair, currently residing at HSR Layout, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Tenant", which expression shall include her heirs and assigns) of the OTHER PART.

WHEREAS the Landlord is the lawful owner of Flat No. 201, Second Floor, Palm Heights, Koramangala 4th Block, Bengaluru (hereinafter called the "Schedule Premises").

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSETH AS FOLLOWS:

1. Term of Tenancy
The tenancy shall be for an initial period of eleven (11) months commencing from 1st October 2026 to 31st August 2027.

2. Monthly Rent
The Tenant agrees to pay a monthly rent of Rs. 25,000/- (Rupees Twenty-Five Thousand only), payable on or before the 5th day of each English calendar month directly into the Landlord's designated bank account.

3. Security Deposit
The Tenant has paid to the Landlord a sum of Rs. 50,000/- (Rupees Fifty Thousand only), representing an interest-free refundable security deposit equivalent to two (2) months' rent.

4. Termination and Notice
Either party may terminate this agreement prior to the expiry of the 11-month term by giving one (1) month of written notice or thirty (30) days' rent in lieu thereof.

5. Permitted Use
The premises shall be used exclusively for residential purposes by the Tenant and her immediate family members. Subletting or assigning the whole or any part of the premises without written consent of the Landlord is strictly prohibited.

6. Utilities and Maintenance
The Tenant shall pay regular electricity and water consumption charges directly to the concerned utility authorities as per actual meter readings.

7. Landlord Inspection
The Landlord or his authorized agent shall have the right to inspect the condition of the premises by giving at least twenty-four (24) hours' prior written notice to the Tenant, during reasonable daytime hours.
